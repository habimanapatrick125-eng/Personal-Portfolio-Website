Who I Am

My Biography

I'm Alex Rivera, a 22-year-old front-end developer and designer currently completing my Bachelor of Science in Digital Media & Web Technologies at the University of Washington. My journey into tech began at age 15 when I built my first HTML page for a school project — the instant I saw my code come alive in a browser, I was hooked.

Today, I specialize in building responsive, accessible, and beautifully designed interfaces using HTML5, CSS3, JavaScript, and Bootstrap 5. I care deeply about the details: micro-interactions, typography, color contrast, and the small touches that make a product feel polished and human.

Beyond the classroom, I've collaborated on real-world projects with student startups, contributed to open-source design systems, and led workshops to help peers learn modern front-end practices. I'm a firm believer that good design is invisible — it simply works.
